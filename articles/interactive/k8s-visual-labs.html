How a Deployment replaces its Pods

A rolling update moves Pods from the old ReplicaSet to the new one. maxSurge caps how many extra Pods may exist, and maxUnavailable caps how many may be missing. Each row below is one controller pass. A new Pod becomes Ready one pass after it is created.

Which Pods does my Service reach?

      
A Service sends traffic to every Pod whose labels match all of its selector pairs and that is Ready. Edit the selector, the Pod labels or readiness, and see the endpoints, the traffic share and what curl would return.

    Who can talk to whom?
    
      
    With no policies, every Pod can reach every Pod. Once a policy selects a Pod for Ingress, only traffic that some rule allows gets in. Policies add up and never conflict. Turn policies on and off and click a cell to see why.
